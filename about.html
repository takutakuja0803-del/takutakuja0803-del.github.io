About

A team built around making things.

TAKUMI Lab is a California-based student-led technology team founded in June 2026. We build and experiment with software, AI tools, mobile applications, web technology, and digital products.

Our work connects consumer technology with hands-on development. Our work includes released applications, completed projects, working prototypes, and projects in active or early development. We aim to turn ideas into working products and improve them through testing and feedback.

Our mission

We turn ideas into working products using AI, software, web technology, and mobile applications. We aim to give young developers and creators a place to contribute to practical projects and learn by building together.

As a small team, we prototype quickly, test new technologies, and refine what we build into useful digital experiences.
