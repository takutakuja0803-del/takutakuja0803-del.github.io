SwifKit

Overview

SwifKit helps developers explore Apple’s native sounds, haptics, colors, motions, SF Symbols, and symbol effects in one place.

Main features

  • Play and compare Apple system sounds
  • Try standard haptics on a real device
  • Review Apple platform colors
  • Explore motion and animation examples
  • Test SF Symbols and symbol effects
  • Compare multiple native Apple experiences in one app
