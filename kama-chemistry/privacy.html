Overview

Kama Chemistry is a relationship compatibility app by TAKUMI Lab. This page describes practices confirmed so far and identifies details still being verified.

TAKUMI Lab has confirmed that it does not collect user data directly through Kama Chemistry. The app does not use Google AdMob or display advertisements.

Changes to this policy

This page will be updated when the remaining data handling details are confirmed or the app's practices change.
