Overview

An application by TAKUMI Lab.

  • Consider sexual chemistry and attraction
  • Learn about individual archetypes
  • Explore energy-center compatibility
  • Reflect on emotional connection
  • Understand relationship dynamics
  • Consider long-term compatibility
