Smart GPA

Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 31, 2026

Overview

Smart GPA does not require you to create an account and does not collect personal information that you directly enter into the app.

Course and GPA data

Course names, credits, grades, and GPA-related information you enter are used to calculate your GPA and manage your Course Library. Based on the current app implementation, this data is stored on your device using local app storage.

TAKUMI Lab does not operate an external server for receiving this course or GPA data.

Advertising and Google AdMob

Smart GPA uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. In connection with providing and measuring ads, Google and its partners may collect, use, and process information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers
  • Device information, including device type and operating system
  • Diagnostics, including crash and performance information
  • Ad interaction and other advertising-related data

Google processes this information under its own policies. For more information, please review the Google Privacy Policy.

Analytics

The current app implementation reviewed for this policy does not include a separate analytics SDK. If analytics are added in the future, this policy will be updated.

Children’s privacy

Smart GPA is intended for college students and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
