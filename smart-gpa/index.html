Overview

An application by TAKUMI Lab.

Smart GPA is a simple GPA calculator for students, listed on the App Store as My GPA. Enter subjects, grades, and credits to calculate your GPA quickly.

Main features

  • Calculate your GPA quickly
  • Enter subjects, grades, and credits
  • Use a simple, easy-to-read design

Availability

Released on the App Store as My GPA. Free for iPhone and iPad. Requires iOS 18.5 or later or iPadOS 18.5 or later.
